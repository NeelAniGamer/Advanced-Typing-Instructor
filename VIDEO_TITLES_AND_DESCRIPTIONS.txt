Advanced Typing Instructor (ATI) is a modern desktop typing tutor and competitive racing game. Built from the ground up with React 18, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, ATI fuses high-speed typing practice with arcade gaming mechanics.

[KEY BULLETS]
- ⌨️ Tactile Typing Engine: Real-time spring-physics caret, live tachometer, spacebar confirmation, and glowing virtual mechanical keycaps.
- 🎧 Web Audio Switch Synthesizer: Cherry MX Blue (clicky), Brown (tactile), and Red (linear) switch acoustics.
- 🏎️ Global Multiplayer Circuit: Zero-config multiplayer powered by Supabase Realtime Cloud (hvukxajztizsuhfubjws) and custom domain relay (advancedlogiclabs.dpdns.org).
- 👤 Persistent Gamer Profiles: SQLite persistence for lifetime stats, win rates, custom avatars, titles, and 10 dynamic rank tiers.

- 📊 Error Heatmap: Real-time visual diagnosis of weak fingers and error clusters.

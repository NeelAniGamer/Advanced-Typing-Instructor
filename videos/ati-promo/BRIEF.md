## Intent

A 30-second vertical promo for Advanced Typing Instructor v2.5 — a free
desktop typing tutor and competitive typing game built by Neel, Ansh & Aarush.
Targets young gamers, students, and professionals who want to get faster at
typing but find traditional tutors boring. The tone is confident and energetic —
closer to a game trailer than a software demo. The video uses text overlays and
funk music only — no voiceover narration.

## Assets

- capture/assets/* — fresh screenshots of the running ATI app (landing page,
  typing arena, achievements, multiplayer, speed test results)

## Customizations

- Counter tick-up on the WPM stat (0 → 142).
- Achievement cards cascade in a waterfall pattern.
- Keyboard burst animation for the opening hook (abstract keys exploding
  outward from center).
- Warm bold palette: coral (#FF5E5B), amber (#FFBE0B), cream (#f5f0e8) on
  charcoal (#1a1a2e) — explicitly NOT cyberpunk/neon/cyan.
- CTA URL: advancedlogiclabs.dpdns.org

## Notes

- No cyan-on-dark, no gradient text, no neon — avoid all AI design tells.
- The video must feel designed by a human, not generated.
- Pattern interrupts every 3-4 seconds for attention retention.
- Velocity shifts: fast motion → sudden freeze → resume.
- Scale punch: elements snap 0 → 120% → 100% with elastic ease.
- The funk beat drops on the very first frame (F01 hook burst).
- Vertical only — no landscape version needed.
- Built by Neel, Ansh & Aarush — credit them in the authority beat.
